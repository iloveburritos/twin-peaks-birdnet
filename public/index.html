    About

    This station is a Raspberry Pi with a USB microphone. It records short stretches of sound, and the BirdNET model from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology suggests which species is singing. Identification by sound is imperfect, so treat each entry as a good guess.

    Detections are published a day after they are heard. Recordings pass through a human-voice filter, and anything that turns out not to be a bird is removed.

    The data is open: download every detection as CSV. Recordings are linked from each row.

    Built on Avian Visitors by Teddy Warner, whose design and bird illustrations this site adapts, on BirdNET-Pi by Patrick McGuire and contributors, and on BirdNET from the K. Lisa Yang Center for Conservation Bioacoustics, Cornell Lab of Ornithology.
